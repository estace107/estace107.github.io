Recent Activities

New Project under Dr. Feng

I will use my newfound nanofabrication experience to fabricate 2D MoS2 resonators for low temperature experiments. This exciting project will combine practical nanofab skills along with an in-depth theoretical study of MEMS resonators.

Best Paper Award at Hilton Head Conference (6/4)

Our paper won the Best Paper Award at the Hilton Head Conference, an honor awarded to only four papers at the conference. You can see award announcement here. Those who won the award are also presented with the opportunity for submission to Nature Microsystems & Nanoengineering.

Hilton Head Conference Presentation (6/2/26)

My graduate student mentor Timothy Caplice will be presenting our work on NEMS Ising machines at the 2026 Hilton Head Workshop on Solid State Sensors, Actuators, and Microsystems. I'm proud to contribute to our group's research in novel computing paradigms.

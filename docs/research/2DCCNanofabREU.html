It was an honor to work under my world-class faculty and graduate mentors, and work with my brilliant peers.

Project Title: Threshold Engineering for 2D Field-Effect Transistors

PI: Prof. Saptarshi Das

The goal of the project was to fabricate MoS2 and WSe2 transistors with a 2nm "liner" layer between the gate and the dieelectric layers to modify the threshold voltage. The materials chosen for the liner layer were:

  • HfN
  • AlN
  • ZrN
  • BN
  • Al2O3
  • ZrO2
  • Ta2O5
  • TiO2

The nitrides are semiconducting and modify the effective work function of the Ti/Pt gate, while the oxides increase the oxide capacitance. These effects can both increase and decrease the threshold voltage. The fabrication process is shown below.

While I've since left State College, the fabrication process is still ongoing. When the devices are finally measured, we anticipate a threshold voltage shift on order of ~1V, consistent with modern CMOS systems.

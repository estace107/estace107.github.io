I was a member of the 2025 UT Dallas Physics and Astronomy REU. Students were paired with individual faculty mentors to study selected topics in physics and astronomy. This was an extremely rewarding intellectual experience, and was my first time conducting full-time physics research.

Project Title: Leveling Up: Resonant Scattering within Multi-Level Atoms

PI: Prof. Aaron Smith

Lyman-alpha radiation dominates the early universe and is one of the most important tools for cosmological studies. Most radiative transfer models only account for Lyman-alpha radiative transfer networks, and neglect higher order transistions. For this project, we added the Atom module to the Cosmic Lyman-alpha Transfer (COLT) code which accounts for these higher order transitions, and created an analytical model that matches simulations our astrophysical regimes of interest. I also gained a lot of professional experience, through a conference presentation at the 2026 APS Global Physics Summit, and a first-author publication in Publications of the Astronomical Society of Australia.
